The game combines post-apocalyptic settlement management, survival city building, exploration, resource gathering, and mini-4X strategy.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **After Inc: Revival**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and progression enhancements for rebuilding settlements, managing resources, exploring regions, developing civilization, and fighting zombie infestations. It is designed around the game's settlement, expedition, resource, stamina, morale, authority, patience, and Fighter systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with settlement layouts, replay campaigns with fewer resource constraints, test civilization strategies, explore Expeditions freely, or reduce the difficulty of zombie defense.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

After Inc: Revival uses a strategic world map rather than direct character movement. Select areas to explore, scavenge, claim, or clear, and use available Stamina to perform manual actions. The trainer's Stamina option is useful when expanding across multiple regions or testing exploration decisions.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a traditional character inventory. Resources are managed through settlement stockpiles, production buildings, scavenging, exploration rewards, and deliveries. Activate resource protection before construction, development, or large-scale expansion to prevent supported materials from being consumed.

### World & Environment

Build and upgrade settlements across a persistent post-apocalyptic map, explore abandoned areas, gather resources, respond to events, and manage society through decisions affecting Morale, Authority, Patience, and population traits. Expeditions add replayable regions, goals, modifiers, rewards, and difficulty levels, while Sandbox mode allows settlement building without Goals or Impatience.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat focuses on training and upgrading Fighters to eliminate zombie infestations and defend humanity. Assign Fighters to dangerous areas, monitor their strength and health, and use the combat options to reduce losses during difficult encounters. The trainer does not add weapons or action-game controls because combat is handled through the game's strategic systems.
